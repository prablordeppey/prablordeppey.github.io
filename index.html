01

Statistical Estimation of Onion Seedling Density (Pwalugu Study)

A statistical sampling framework for estimating onion seedling density in data-scarce smallholder farming systems in Pwalugu, Upper East Region of Ghana. Using field observations from a two-acre farm and the Central Limit Theorem, the work estimates mean planting density and quantifies variability in manual transplanting practices — providing a practical, low-cost planning model for seedling requirements based on effective bed area.

Dataset Foundry

A synthetic data generation platform for researchers, students, and analysts who need documented, defensible datasets when real data is scarce, restricted, or doesn’t exist yet. Each domain is its own "line family" with a documented rationale behind every field, its own hypothesis table, and its own methodology page. 01

Simuclustfactor

Implements the T3Clus and 3FKMeans iterative techniques for simultaneously clustering objects and reducing the dimensionality of variables and occasions on three-mode datasets (Vichi et al., 2007), plus a convex combination of the two — CT3Clus — governed by a hyperparameter α ∈ [0,1] (3FKMeans at α=0, T3Clus at α=1). Also implements the traditional tandem procedures TWCFTA and TWFCTA (Arabie & Hubert, 1996) for sequential clustering-factorial decomposition.

Thesis Summaries

01

Bachelors Thesis — Willingness To Pay With Social Interactions: A Case Study of Ghana’s NHIS (2019)

A case study on improving National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) coverage in the Sunyani municipality, using the Contingent Valuation Method (CVM) to estimate individuals’ Willingness To Pay (WTP) for extended coverage. Binary Logit Regression, including a social interaction term, identified factors influencing the decision to pay. Findings showed most NHIS holders who purchase additional drugs were willing to pay for extended coverage — most notably for rehabilitation, cancer, and heart-related conditions, averaging up to GH₵30.00/year — and recommended policymakers weigh income, gender, and education in coverage decisions.

RolesTeam Member · Supervisor: Dr. Alex A. Opoku
DocumentDownload file
02

Masters Dissertation — Simultaneous Factorial Reduction and Clustering Models for Three-Mode Datasets (2022)

Developed and implemented five models — TWCFTA, TWFCTA, T3Clus, 3FKMeans, and CT3Clus — in the simuclustfactor packages (Python and R) for analyzing three-mode datasets, proposed by Vichi et al. (2007) as an alternative to traditional tandem models. Tested on synthetic and real datasets, the simultaneous models produced more well-separated, higher-cohesion clusters and better recovered object-cluster and variable/occasion-factor associations than tandem models, with the best results obtained when factorial analysis was prioritized.

RolesUA Supervisor: Prof. Adelaide Freitas · AQ Supervisor: Prof. Alessandro Giovannelli
